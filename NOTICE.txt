# External renderer provenance

The native option uses a precompiled third-party PES6 renderer from the
PES6-NOBK-Online 1.3.3 package supplied locally by the user. Its renderer source
is not included and is not authored by PR7. PR7 supplies the compatibility
extension, editor integration, presentation changes and local modifications.

The executable's icon and product labels identify this integration. They do
not change the provenance or licenses of its components. Retain upstream
license notices. ReXGlue/Xenia, re:Blue/Plume, MinHook and AMD FidelityFX retain
their respective notices where supplied.

Local environment names are PES6_ONLINE_TOKEN, PES6_PATCH and PES6_VOICE.
Upstream network protocol identifiers, exported C++ symbols and original
source-path diagnostic strings remain unchanged for binary compatibility.
These remaining identifiers are not PR7 branding.

The proprietary game data, user accounts, tokens, saves and external renderer
binaries are local inputs, not project source. Do not include them in a source
release. No redistribution permission for the external renderer is established
by changing its icon or labels.
